02 / Hospitality website

Myst Hookah Lounge

Lounge website with menu & reservations
Client project 100+ menu items Private, screenshots only

A dark, cinematic one-page site for a hookah lounge: an intro film, a hookah that follows you down the page, a full food, drinks and flavor menu, and a reservation flow, all in a gold-on-black theme with a sandy grain texture.

  • Intro video that hands off to a 360° rotating hookah rendered on canvas, with live smoke particles above the coals
  • The hookah travels with the scroll, docking beside each section and changing its glow colour to match
  • Tabbed menu in a zigzag layout joined by an animated SVG path; photos load automatically per dish
  • 63 hookah flavors with instant search, plus full price lists for bites, plates, desserts and drinks
  • Reservation form that checks availability and suggests the nearest free time slots
